Summary

  • 6 schools with value added for all pupils averaging -0.8 points against England: none significantly above significantly above expectation and none below.

  • Girls: -0.7 points; boys: -0.8.
  • Strongest: St Catherine’s College (+1.4). Weakest: Gildredge House (-2.4).

What explains the area’s results

For all pupils, Eastbourne’s two largest pieces are prior attainment (KS2) (-2.1) and region (-0.5), and its schools themselves account for -0.9 points. For disadvantaged pupils the two largest are prior attainment (KS2) (-1.4) and region (-1.2), and the schools -1.0.

Notes on method

Value added v2.0 is described on the method page. School districts come from each school’s postcode. The decomposition splits each area’s mean log Attainment 8 against England’s into one piece per model term, exactly, and converts to points at England’s mean.
