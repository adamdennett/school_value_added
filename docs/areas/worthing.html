Worthing

Summary

  • 6 schools with value added for all pupils averaging +1.6 points against England: 2 significantly above significantly above expectation and none below.

  • Girls: +0.9 points; boys: +2.6.
  • Strongest: St Oscar Romero Catholic School (+4.7). Weakest: Bohunt School Worthing (-1.6).

What explains the area’s results

For all pupils, Worthing’s two largest pieces are absence (+0.8) and disadvantage (+0.6), and its schools themselves account for +1.8 points. For disadvantaged pupils the two largest are region (-1.2) and local authority (-1.0), and the schools +2.2.

Notes on method

Value added v2.0 is described on the method page. School districts come from each school’s postcode. The decomposition splits each area’s mean log Attainment 8 against England’s into one piece per model term, exactly, and converts to points at England’s mean.
