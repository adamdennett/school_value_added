Mid Sussex

Value Added v2.0 for the secondary schools of Mid Sussex, West Sussex.

NoteAbout this page

Value Added v2.0 for Mid Sussex’s state-funded secondary schools, 2021/22 to 2024/25, measured against England: see the method. The original analysis, with absence, suspensions and trusts in depth, is here. Built from published DfE and Ofsted data; nothing uses pupil-level records.

Summary

  • 7 schools with value added for all pupils averaging +0.6 points against England: 1 significantly above significantly above expectation and none below.
  • Disadvantaged pupils: -2.6 points; non-disadvantaged pupils: +0.5.
  • Girls: +0.7 points; boys: +0.1.
  • Strongest: Downlands Community School (+3.0). Weakest: St Paul’s Catholic College (-0.7).
  • Against the published v1 measure, the area’s schools move by -0.4 points on average (v2 against England minus v1 within the authority).
  • Latest Ofsted: 2 report cards, 1 graded inspection and 4 ungraded visit only.

What explains the area’s results

For all pupils, Mid Sussex’s two largest pieces are disadvantage (+1.5) and prior attainment (KS2) (+0.7), and its schools themselves account for +0.9 points. For disadvantaged pupils the two largest are region (-1.2) and local authority (-1.0), and the schools -1.3.

Notes on method

Value added v2.0 is described on the method page. School districts come from each school’s postcode. The decomposition splits each area’s mean log Attainment 8 against England’s into one piece per model term, exactly, and converts to points at England’s mean.
