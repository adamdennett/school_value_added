Arun

Value Added v2.0 for the secondary schools of Arun, West Sussex.

NoteAbout this page

Value Added v2.0 for Arun’s state-funded secondary schools, 2021/22 to 2024/25, measured against England (see the method), with the analyses of the original Sussex document refitted on the v2.0 model. Built from published DfE and Ofsted data; nothing uses pupil-level records.

Summary

  • 6 schools, with value added for all pupils averaging -0.2 points against England: 2 significantly above expectation and 1 below.
  • Disadvantaged pupils -1.7, non-disadvantaged -0.5; girls -0.3, boys -0.1.
  • Strongest: St Philip Howard Catholic School (+2.5). Weakest: The Littlehampton Academy (-3.0).
  • Absence runs 0.2 points above what the intake predicts on average over 2021/22 to 2024/25; The Littlehampton Academy more than a point above.
  • No district effect can be found on the v2.0 model.
  • Suspensions: 19.3 episodes per 100 pupils (England 21.2); 6.5 pupils per 100 suspended at least once (England 7.3). Reduced timetables run at 0.9 times the national rate.
  • Trusts that stand out nationally on v2.0: United Learning Trust (all pupils +7.3% and disadvantaged pupils +8.6%).
  • Latest Ofsted: 2 report cards, 2 graded inspections and 2 ungraded visits only.
  • 2025/26, provisional: value added for all pupils averages +0.4 points across the area’s schools on DfE’s provisional results. This will be updated with the revised release in February 2027.

How Arun compares

What explains attainment in Arun

For all pupils, Arun is 3.3 points below England. The pieces of at least a quarter of a point:

  • prior attainment (KS2) (-1.64);
  • neighbourhood education (-0.84);
  • absence (-0.80);
  • disadvantage (+0.54);
  • region (-0.40);
  • and the schools themselves (-0.04).

For disadvantaged pupils, Arun is 5.5 points below England. The pieces of at least a quarter of a point:

  • region (-1.22);
  • prior attainment (KS2) (-1.10);
  • local authority (-1.04);
  • absence (-0.86);
  • admissions (selection) (-0.27);
  • disadvantage (-0.26);
  • and the schools themselves (-0.26).

Place: authorities and districts

The district test adds a district level to the v2.0 model, with and without each school’s own record. An effect that disappears once schools are counted belongs to particular schools; one that survives is something the district’s schools share.

None of Arun has a district effect distinguishable from zero on the v2.0 model.

Trusts

The v2.0 model with a multi-academy trust level, allowing for each school’s own record and for how it came to be an academy, so trusts that take on struggling schools are not penalised for it. Nationally a trust moves results by about 3.0%, and absence by about 6.5%.

The disadvantaged gap, and girls and boys

St Philip Howard Catholic School is above expectation for non-disadvantaged pupils and more than a point below it for disadvantaged ones. The Regis School is above expectation for both groups. Girls’ and boys’ value added differ by more than two points at Ormiston Six Villages Academy (girls -1.7, boys +1.2).

Absence

  • 8.7% absence in autumn and spring 2025/26 (9.1% a year earlier), against 8.0% for England.
  • Against its intake, on the v2.0 intake terms, the area’s schools ran 0.18 points above what their pupils’ profiles predict over 2021/22 to 2024/25 (West Sussex -0.27 and Brighton and Hove +1.64).
  • The Littlehampton Academy runs more than a point above its prediction.
  • St Philip Howard Catholic School runs more than a point below.

Suspensions were 19.3 episodes per 100 pupils in 2024/25, against 21.2 for England. One pupil can be suspended several times: 6.5 pupils per 100 were suspended at least once, about 3.0 times each, against 7.3 per 100 and 2.9 times in England. Reduced timetables take 0.27% of sessions, 0.9 times England’s rate.

School by school

Across England the two signals move together: schools whose absence runs below what their intake predicts tend to add more. Across Arun’s schools the correlation between the two is -0.93 (-0.55 across England).

Questions for discussion

  1. Disadvantaged pupils. Their value added averages -1.7 points across Arun’s schools against -0.5 for other pupils. What does The Regis School, above expectation for them, do that others could share?
  2. Attendance is a modest lever. Absence costs 0.8 points against England.
  3. Schools, not the place. No district effect survives each school’s own record on v2.0: the differences that matter are between schools.
  4. Practice. Reduced timetables and suspensions are close to or below the national rate.
  5. The authority. West Sussex’s own v2.0 effect for disadvantaged pupils is -2.6% (124th of 151), distinguishable from zero.

Notes on method

Value Added v2.0 is described on the method page. The absence-against-intake measure, the district test, the trust analysis, the attendance-counted-in value added and the scenarios are refitted on the v2.0 specification by R/04_place_and_absence.R. The absence series, reasons and suspensions are the original project’s (R/17_area_analysis.R). School districts come from each school’s postcode. The decomposition splits each area’s mean log Attainment 8 against England’s into one piece per model term, exactly, and converts to points at England’s mean. This page is generated from templates/area.qmd by R/03_area_pages.R; the format is recorded in AREA_REPORTS.md.
