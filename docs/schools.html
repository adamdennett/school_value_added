Every school

Value Added v2.0 for every state-funded mainstream secondary school in England, by pupil group.

Value added is in Attainment 8 points against England, averaged over a school’s years (2021/22 to 2024/25) and shrunk by its reliability. A school whose interval for all pupils lies wholly above or below zero is marked. Search by name, town or authority, and sort by any column. The full data, with intervals for every group and the within-authority version, are in va_v2_schools.csv. Ofsted is the latest judgement in Ofsted’s data as at 30 September 2026.

Adam Dennett · Bartlett Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis, UCL
