Sussex

Value Added v2.0 across East Sussex, West Sussex and Brighton and Hove.

NoteAbout this page

Value Added v2.0 for Sussex’s state-funded secondary schools, 2021/22 to 2024/25, measured against England: see the method. The original analysis, with absence, suspensions and trusts in depth, is here. Built from published DfE and Ofsted data; nothing uses pupil-level records.

Summary

  • 76 schools with value added for all pupils averaging +0.2 points against England: 18 significantly above significantly above expectation and 8 below.
  • Disadvantaged pupils: -1.0 points; non-disadvantaged pupils: +0.1.
  • Girls: +0.0 points; boys: +0.4.
  • Strongest: Priory School (+5.0). Weakest: The St Leonards Academy (-5.5).
  • Against the published v1 measure, the area’s schools move by +0.1 points on average (v2 against England minus v1 within the authority).
  • Latest Ofsted: 18 report cards, 12 inspection with no overall grades, 28 graded inspections and 18 ungraded visit only.

What explains the area’s results

For all pupils, Sussex’s two largest pieces are absence (-0.7) and local authority (+0.6), and its schools themselves account for +0.1 points. For disadvantaged pupils the two largest are region (-1.2) and absence (-0.7), and the schools +0.0.

Notes on method

Value added v2.0 is described on the method page. School districts come from each school’s postcode. The decomposition splits each area’s mean log Attainment 8 against England’s into one piece per model term, exactly, and converts to points at England’s mean.
